# VIII. Education Policy

Summary

Federal-led reforms to equalize education funding, improve outcomes, and expand access to higher education while supporting teachers and research institutions.

Policy items

- Federal Equalization — Replace property-tax-based funding with federal equalization grants to ensure minimum per-student resources nationwide.
- Teacher Compensation & Training — National guidelines for salary floors, professional development, and recruitment incentives.
- Early Childhood Expansion — Universal pre-K and expanded childcare subsidies to improve early learning outcomes.
- Remedial & Targeted Support — Increased funding for tutoring, summer programs, and special education to close achievement gaps.
- Higher Education Affordability — Eliminate tuition at public institutions through federal-state partnerships and increase Pell Grants.
- Student Loan Relief & Reform — Cancel interest accrual on federal loans and restructure repayment around income-driven schedules.
- Research Funding — Boost federal research funding to universities and strengthen technology transfer with public-interest safeguards.

Implementation & Administration

Primary agencies: Department of Education, NSF, and state education agencies for implementation. Metrics: graduation rates, per-pupil spending equality indices, teacher retention rates, college enrollment and completion, and research funding outputs. Implementation will require phased funding adjustments, federal-state agreements, and targeted grant programs with accountability metrics tied to outcomes.

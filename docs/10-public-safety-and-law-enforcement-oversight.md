# X. Public Safety and Law Enforcement Oversight

Summary

Reforms to ensure accountable, community-centered public safety through independent oversight, revised liability standards, and professionalization of policing.

Policy items

- Independent Investigatory Body — Establish or empower a federal independent body to investigate civil rights abuses by law enforcement.
- Qualified Immunity Reform — Modify legal doctrines to permit civil accountability for clear constitutional violations.
- Certification & Decertification — National standards for training, licensing, and decertification of officers who commit serious misconduct.
- Liability Insurance & Accountability — Require professional liability insurance and strengthen municipal accountability mechanisms.
- Community-Led Safety Programs — Invest in non-police crisis response teams for mental health and substance use incidents.
- Data Transparency — National reporting requirements for use-of-force incidents, stops, and complaints with public dashboards.
- Training & Standards — Federal grants to support de-escalation, bias training, and community policing reforms tied to measurable outcomes.

Implementation & Administration

Key implementers include the Department of Justice (Civil Rights Division), Department of Homeland Security for federal standards, and state licensing boards. Metrics: independent investigations completed, decertification counts, complaint resolution timelines, and rates of use-of-force incidents. Phased implementation should prioritize pilot oversight structures, federal funding incentives, and statutory clarification where necessary.

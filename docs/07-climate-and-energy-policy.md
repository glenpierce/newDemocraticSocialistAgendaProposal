# VII. Climate and Energy Policy

Summary

A comprehensive industrial and climate policy to rapidly decarbonize the economy, build resilient infrastructure, and create quality jobs in clean energy industries.

Policy items

- Renewable Deployment Targets — Set national targets and timetables for renewable energy adoption across sectors.
- Clean Energy Investment — Federal financing, tax incentives, and direct procurement to scale domestic clean-technology manufacturing.
- Just Transition Programs — Worker retraining, wage insurance, and regional economic development for fossil-fuel-dependent communities.
- Grid Modernization — Invest in transmission, storage, and smart-grid technologies to accommodate high renewable penetrations.
- Emissions Standards — Strengthen sectoral emissions standards for power, transport, and industry aligned with national climate goals.
- Climate Resilience — Federal grants and planning support for adaptation in vulnerable communities and critical infrastructure.
- Research & Development — Increased funding through DOE, NSF, and ARPA-E for breakthrough clean-energy technologies.

Implementation & Administration

Lead agencies include the Department of Energy, Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Transportation, and Department of Labor for workforce measures. Metrics: greenhouse gas emissions, share of electricity from renewables, jobs created in clean industries, and resilience funding disbursed. Implementation will use a mix of regulations, grants, procurement, and tax policy with clear transition timelines and measures to protect affected workers and regions.

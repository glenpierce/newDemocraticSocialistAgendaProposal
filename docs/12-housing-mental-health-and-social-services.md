# XII. Housing, Mental Health, and Social Services

Summary

Integrated policies to expand affordable housing, embed mental health and addiction services into primary care, and coordinate social services for long-term support.

Policy items

- Expand Public & Supportive Housing — Large-scale construction and acquisition programs to increase deeply affordable and supportive housing stock.
- Rental Assistance & Eviction Protections — Strengthen vouchers, rent control where appropriate, and legal support to prevent displacement.
- Integrated Mental Health Care — Co-locate mental health and addiction services in primary care settings with payment reforms to fund integrated treatment.
- Long-Term Care & Community Supports — Expand home- and community-based services and supportive employment programs.
- Homelessness Prevention — Targeted interventions combining housing-first approaches with supportive services and rapid rehousing.
- Interagency Coordination — Create federal-state-local mechanisms to coordinate housing, health, and social service funding and case management.
- Data & Outcomes — Standardize metrics for housing stability, treatment engagement, and service integration outcomes.

Implementation & Administration

Lead agencies: Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD), Health and Human Services (HHS), and state/local health and housing authorities. Metrics: number of affordable units produced, homelessness counts, treatment retention rates, and housing stability outcomes. Implementation will use a combination of direct federal investment, block grants, performance-based contracting, and strengthened local capacity-building initiatives.

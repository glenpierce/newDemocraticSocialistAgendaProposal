# V. Rule of Law and Institutional Accountability

Summary

Strengthening investigation, oversight, and legal accountability of public officials and agencies to protect the rule of law and public trust.

Policy items

- Independent Investigations — Create or strengthen independent inspector generals and special counsels for impartial probes of misconduct.
- Prosecution Standards — Clarify standards for referring and prosecuting official misconduct to ensure consistency and due process.
- Limits on Executive Clemency — Policy and statutory guardrails to prevent obstruction of judicial processes through pardons or commutations.
- Whistleblower Protections — Expand protections, secure reporting channels, and provide remedies and rewards for validated disclosures.
- Oversight Capacity — Increase funding and authority for congressional oversight offices, IGs, and Inspectors General to audit and review agencies.
- Transparency & Records — Mandate timely public release of non-sensitive records, FOIA improvements, and penalties for unlawful withholding.
- Ethics Enforcement — Strengthen ethics offices across branches and require regular disclosure of conflicts of interest.

Implementation & Administration

Key implementers include the Department of Justice, Office of Inspector General offices, Congress (through oversight committees), and independent ethics bodies. Metrics: number of investigations opened/completed, time-to-resolution, FOIA response times, and disciplinary actions taken. Implementation should include statutory clarifications, funding increases for oversight offices, and standardized protocols for cross-agency cooperation.

# IX. Transportation and Traffic Safety

Summary

Policies to align transportation systems with safety, emissions, and equity goals through infrastructure investment, regulation, and technology deployment.

Policy items

- Emissions-Linked Standards — Close loopholes and align vehicle standards with fuel-efficiency and emissions targets.
- Public Transit Investment — Prioritize funding for high-quality transit in underserved corridors and urban centers.
- Traffic-Calming Infrastructure — Invest in protected bike lanes, pedestrian improvements, and road diets to reduce speed-related injuries.
- Automated Enforcement — Expand data-driven automated enforcement (speed, red-light) where evidence shows safety benefits and safeguards are in place.
- Freight & Logistics Modernization — Shift freight to lower-emission modes and upgrade ports, rail, and intermodal facilities.
- EV Infrastructure — Build nationwide charging networks with equitable site selection and interoperability standards.
- Data & Performance Metrics — Standardize safety and emissions data collection to guide local and federal investments.

Implementation & Administration

Lead agencies: Department of Transportation, National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), and state/local transportation departments. Metrics: traffic fatalities and serious injuries, transit ridership, emissions per passenger-mile, EV charger deployment, and project completion timelines. Implementation will combine federal grants (formula and competitive), regulatory updates, and performance-based funding tied to safety and emissions outcomes.

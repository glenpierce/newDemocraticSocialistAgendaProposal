# Platform for a Democratic Socialist Government

**Policy Framework and Legislative Priorities**

## Introduction

The United States has the productive capacity, technical expertise, and financial resources to provide economic security, political stability, and broad prosperity. Persistent inequality, institutional dysfunction, and declining public trust are the result of policy design choices, not structural inevitabilities.

This platform outlines a comprehensive program of economic reform, democratic reconstruction, and public investment intended to restore institutional capacity and align governance with the public interest.

---

## I. Fiscal Policy and Wealth Concentration

### Wealth Taxation

The party supports an **annual 8 percent tax on net assets exceeding $100 million**.

Implementation will include:

* Comprehensive asset disclosure requirements
* Consolidated treatment of trusts, shell corporations, and offshore holdings
* Valuation standards enforced by a specialized federal unit
* Automatic information sharing between tax authorities and financial institutions

Revenue will be directed toward healthcare, education, infrastructure, and scientific research.

### Wages and Income Policy

* Increase the federal minimum wage to a level consistent with regional cost-of-living data
* Index the minimum wage to inflation through automatic adjustment
* Expand enforcement capacity for wage theft, misclassification, and unpaid overtime

### Universal Basic Income

The party supports a phased introduction of **Universal Basic Income**, beginning with pilot programs and expanding based on measured economic and labor-market effects.

---

## II. Healthcare System Reform

### Universal Public Healthcare

The party supports the establishment of **Medicare for All** as a single-payer national healthcare system.

Key components:

* Elimination of private health insurance markets for covered services
* Universal enrollment
* Comprehensive coverage including primary care, hospitalization, mental health, prescription drugs, and long-term care
* Centralized negotiation of pharmaceutical prices

Healthcare financing will be decoupled from employment.

---

## III. Democratic Reform and Electoral Integrity

### Campaign Finance

* Overturn the legal framework established by *Citizens United*
* Establish federally funded elections
* Prohibit corporate and high-net-worth individual campaign contributions

### Legislative Drafting

* Transfer primary legislative drafting authority to professional, nonpartisan policy staff
* Require public documentation of research sources and economic assumptions
* Limit the role of lobbyists to public testimony and written submissions

---

## IV. Structural Reform of Federal Government

### Supreme Court Reform

* Establish fixed terms for Supreme Court justices
* Require compliance with a binding judicial ethics code
* Limit presidential nominations to one justice per term
* Require congressional votes on all nominations within a defined timeframe

### Legislative Structure

* Replace the bicameral legislature with a unicameral body
* Apportion representation strictly by population
* Transition toward a parliamentary-style system in which executive leadership is accountable to the legislature

### Voting System Reform

* Eliminate first-past-the-post voting
* Implement ranked-choice voting nationwide
* Establish snap elections when governments fail confidence votes or core legislative thresholds

---

## V. Rule of Law and Institutional Accountability

The party supports the investigation and prosecution of criminal violations committed by public officials, federal agencies, and associated actors.

* Prosecutorial decisions will be based on evidentiary standards and statutory authority
* Executive clemency powers will not be used to obstruct judicial proceedings
* Oversight mechanisms will be strengthened to prevent future abuses of authority

---

## VI. Competition, Labor Rights, and Market Regulation

### Antitrust Enforcement

* Expand federal antitrust capacity
* Restore structural remedies, including breakups
* Prohibit anticompetitive mergers and predatory pricing

### Labor Protection

* Enforce penalties for union suppression
* Expand collective bargaining rights
* Increase penalties for wage theft and retaliation

### Intellectual Property Reform

* Limit abusive patent litigation
* Reform intellectual property law to prioritize innovation and public benefit

---

## VII. Climate and Energy Policy

### Green New Deal

The party supports a comprehensive climate and industrial policy that includes:

* Rapid transition to renewable energy
* Federal investment in energy infrastructure
* Job creation tied to domestic manufacturing and skilled labor
* Climate resilience planning at the federal, state, and local levels

---

## VIII. Education Policy

### K–12 Education

* Replace property-tax-based funding with federal equalization
* Increase funding for remedial education, tutoring, and summer instruction
* Reduce student-to-teacher ratios
* Raise teacher compensation and establish national salary standards

### Higher Education

* Eliminate tuition at public colleges and universities
* Increase federal support for research institutions
* Cancel interest accrual on existing federal student loans

---

## IX. Transportation and Traffic Safety

* Close fuel-efficiency loopholes related to vehicle classification
* Expand automated traffic enforcement where supported by safety data
* Invest in traffic-calming infrastructure and public transit
* Align transportation policy with emissions and safety targets

---

## X. Public Safety and Law Enforcement Oversight

* Establish an independent federal body to investigate police misconduct
* Reform qualified immunity to allow civil accountability for constitutional violations
* Require professional liability insurance for law enforcement officers
* Expand training, certification, and decertification standards

---

## XI. Science, Technology, and Public Ownership

* Increase federal funding for scientific research
* Require public ownership or licensing of technologies developed with public funds
* Prevent exclusive privatization of publicly financed pharmaceutical research

---

## XII. Housing, Mental Health, and Social Services

* Expand public housing and supportive housing programs
* Integrate mental health and addiction treatment into primary care systems
* Establish long-term treatment pathways for individuals requiring intensive support
* Coordinate housing, healthcare, and social services at the federal level

## Conclusion

This platform is designed to restore institutional capacity, reduce inequality, and align public policy with democratic governance. Its proposals emphasize enforceability, administrative feasibility, and long-term sustainability.

The objective is a government that functions predictably, governs competently, and serves the public interest.
